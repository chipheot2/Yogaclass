✦ About Me

Hi, I'm Ha Nguyen

I discovered yoga during a period of burnout and chronic stress, and what began as a physical practice quickly became something much deeper — a complete reorientation of how I relate to myself, my body, and the world around me.

After completing my 200-hour Vinyasa Yoga Teacher Training, I knew teaching was my calling. Every class I lead carries the same intention: to create a safe, non-judgmental space where students can breathe, move, and simply be.

My teaching is shaped by the way I live — I'm a hiker, a runner, and a scuba diver. Movement is woven into who I am. This means I understand how real bodies feel after a long run, a tough hike, or a day in the water, and I design my yoga classes to complement an active life, not compete with it.

Whether you're looking to recover faster, move more freely, or simply find stillness after adventure — I'm here for all of it.
